# CoachRoyale App Icon V2

## Direction

The icon uses a simplified crown-shield hybrid with a small upward strategy chevron. The gold mark cues CoachRoyale and ranked mastery, while the cyan accent carries the coaching/analysis signal without becoming a dominant checkmark.

## Palette

- Deep navy: `#101827`
- Royal gold: `#F6C343`
- Electric cyan: `#22D3EE`
- Slate depth: `#18243D`

## Guardrails

- No claw mark: that belongs to repo/legacy `Declaw` naming, not the app.
- No Supercell, Clash Royale, character, tower, card, sword, or arena-derived imagery.
- No text inside the icon; it needs to work at favicon size.
- Keep the silhouette simple: one primary gold mark plus one small cyan accent.
